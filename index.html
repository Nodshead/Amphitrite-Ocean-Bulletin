Custom vessel profile

A great value here is that we can add your custom vessel profile to the platform. Not a generic one — one built from your vessel's actual data: speed through water, speed over ground, heading, COG, position logs, hull dimensions, engine power, and any additional performance data available on board.

That profile is then used at the heart of the routing — on top of our already high-resolution current, wave, and wind data. With a custom ML-trained model, we get to less than 5% mean average percentage error on speed predictions.

How we get our data
1
Meteorological and satellite data. SSH, SST, SWOT, and chlorophyll imagery from global observation programmes and satellite networks.
2
Processed through HIRES-CURRENTS, our deep learning AI — built to model ocean dynamics at high resolution.
3
Validated against in-situ measurements. Buoys, drifters, and direct ocean sensors — independent of the data used during training.
4
Accurate wind, waves, and ocean currents at 3 km resolution (1/30°), updated hourly — all in one platform.
